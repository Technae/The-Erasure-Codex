# Contributing

This guide only stays useful if it stays accurate. Discord's handling changes, and each new report helps people know what to expect.

## Share your experience

Open an issue using the **Experience report** template. The most useful details are:

- **When:** the month you sent your request
- **Where you live:** the country, which decides your authority and whether the GDPR or UK GDPR applies
- **What you sent:** email or support form, and roughly how many messages
- **What Discord replied,** quoted exactly, with dates
- **What was actually deleted,** ideally as a before/after comparison from the helper script
- **What happened next:** complaints, regulator responses, anything that worked

You don't need to share your username, user ID, ticket numbers or anything else that identifies you unless you want to.

## Suggest a correction

If something is wrong, out of date or unclear, open an issue or a pull request. For anything legal, please link to the source: the law itself, a regulator's guidance or decision, or Discord's own published text.

## Ground rules

- **Quote Discord exactly.** Paraphrases drift. Put Discord's words in quotation marks and give the date.
- **Separate facts from opinions.** "Discord's first reply said bulk deletion isn't offered" is a fact. "Discord is lying" is an opinion. Both have a place, but readers (and regulators) need to tell them apart.
- **Don't post other people's personal data.** Remove names, usernames, user IDs and message content of anyone else from screenshots and logs. Black boxes, not blur.
- **Don't post your own sensitive data either.** Channel names, message content and the per-channel report from the helper script can reveal a lot.
- **No account automation.** This guide is about legal requests. Contributions that rely on self-bots or tokens won't be accepted.
- **Be kind.** People arrive here frustrated. Help them.

## Translations

Translations are very welcome, since most EU users write to their authority in their own language. Put them in `docs/<language-code>/` (for example `docs/fr/`), and keep the templates' article numbers unchanged.
